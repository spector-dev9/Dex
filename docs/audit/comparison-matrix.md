## Source-of-truth gaps

The supplied `plan.md` ends at §5.3 but cites requirements in §§6, 10 and
11 and test IDs (including SC, WS, TM, RT, RL, and ST groups) whose full
procedures and acceptance criteria are not present in that file. Preserve
those IDs as traceability targets, but do not invent thresholds from the
lower-priority `docs/spec.md`. Complete the authoritative plan before
freezing those gates or implementing behavior whose acceptance depends on
them.

The app's `minSdk` is now 26 to match §4.1 of `plan.md`; the resulting APK
still needs runtime/device validation before that support floor is
considered verified.
